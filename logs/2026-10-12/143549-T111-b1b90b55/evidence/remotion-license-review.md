# Remotion license review

確認日: 2026-10-12

公式FAQ: https://www.remotion.dev/docs/license/faq

確認した記載:
- Free License eligibility: individual, whether for personal or commercial use; organizations/teams up to 3; non-profits; pre-commercial evaluation.
- FAQ「Can I make money under the Free License?」: Yes.
- Single-person companies can use Free License; an incorporated organization remains eligible with total headcount 3 or less.
- Any commercial use is allowed subject to the stated restrictions (including not selling Remotion as a product or enabling license circumvention).
- Organizations not eligible for Free License need Company License. The FAQ categorizes manual video creation as Remotion for Creators and automation products/rendering services as Remotion for Automators.

判断: 個人が自分の収益化YouTubeチャンネル用動画を制作する想定では、収益化は商用利用だがFree Licenseの個人枠に該当しCompany License不要。会社・チームの利用では規模と実際の使い方を再確認する。

制約: FAQの要約を確認したもので、正式なTerms and Conditions全文の法的解釈は行っていない。規約の正式定義はTerms and Conditionsが優先。
